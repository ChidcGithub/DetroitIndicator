What is exact

  • 1920 × 1080 stage, safeY = −42; the strip is 346 × 150 with four 173 × 150 triangles.
  • Name right edge 1862, adjective insert at y = 56, state anchor at (1902, 34), padlock centre (1886, 124).
  • 30 fps frame strobes, 8-frame adjective flicker, 1500 ms hold, 500 ms fade; 1000 ms Quint.easeOut chevrons and Strong.easeOut padlock halves.
  • The 64 × 96 arrow is the GFX pentagon (0,32) (−32,0) (−32,64) (0,96) (32,64) (32,0), used twice as artwork and as its own moving mask.
  • The main canvas shows the complete group, including its 42 px top bleed and 22 px right bleed. The map shows the original 1920 × 1080 position.

What is deliberately not faked

  • The background is not a texture. It is drawn from the AS3 triangle coordinates; the only bitmaps are the two padlock halves (and the unused success chevron).
  • No sound is played: the gauge dispatches named Scaleform events, but the audio bank was not located. The event name is shown instead.
  • The shared fonts/FONT_MASTER_ENG.swf is external to the gauge bundle and is not in the extracted data, so text is rasterized with Bahnschrift / Arial Narrow at the decoded sizes with 10 px tracking to match the shipped font's spacing. The wording itself comes from the extracted localization table.
  • Positive/UNLOCKED blue is #80EEFF, the shipped UI-GFX blue. The shared QDColor.BLUE literal is #FCA2FF and does not match the shipped gauge, so it is not used. Negative red is QDColor.RED = #FF3000.
  • The LOCKED state uses the same $GUI_GAUGE_UNLOCK caption and unlock pose in the decompiled class; only the plate tint and sound event differ. The demo keeps that quirk.
